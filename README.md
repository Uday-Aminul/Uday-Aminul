### 🚀 What I've built

**[BAUST Class Routine Management System](https://github.com/Uday-Aminul/BAUST-Class-Routine-Managment)**  
A production timetable system in active use by the CSE department at BAUST, replacing a fully manual routine-creation process. I designed and built the entire backend — including a **custom constraint-satisfaction scheduling algorithm** that resolves teacher availability conflicts, classroom/lab clashes, and alternating-week lab sessions, with a slot-swap mechanism to free up unavailable slots without creating new conflicts.  
`C#` · `ASP.NET Core` · `EF Core` · `MS SQL` · `React` · `Docker Compose` · `GitHub Actions CI`

**[TrackNStock — Inventory Management API](https://github.com/Uday-Aminul/TrackNStock)**

`C#` · `ASP.NET Core` · `EF Core` · `MS SQL` · `JWT`
